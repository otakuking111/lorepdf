Blue Monster Run

The Blue Monster is chasing you. Jump over obstacles, collect glowing power orbs, and survive long enough to face it.

During a fight: use Bolt to attack. Collect Flight for automatic shots or Shield to block a hit. Switch powers with Q or ⇄.

Keyboard: Jump: Space / ↑. Attack: Enter / F. Switch power: Q. Pause: Esc / P.

Phone controls: Tap the screen or ↑ to jump. During a boss fight, tap ✦ to attack. Hold your phone sideways for a wider, clearer view.
