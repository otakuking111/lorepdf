The Hobbit, or There and Back Again

by J.R.R. Tolkien

FantasyAdventureClassic LiteratureEpic Fantasy

Bilbo Baggins is a comfort-loving Hobbit whose peaceful life changes when the wizard Gandalf and thirteen dwarves led by Thorin Oakenshield invite him on an unexpected adventure. Their mission is to reclaim the Lonely Mountain and its lost treasure from the dragon Smaug. Along the way, Bilbo faces trolls, goblins, giant spiders, and other dangers. Deep within the Misty Mountains, he encounters Gollum and discovers a mysterious ring that will later play a major role in the fate of Middle-earth.
