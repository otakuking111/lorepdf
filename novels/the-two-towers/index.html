Starting Plot

The Fellowship is broken after a fierce battle; Frodo and Sam continue their journey to Mount Doom alone, while unknowingly tracked by the creature Gollum.

Main Conflict

The remaining members of the broken fellowship (Aragorn, Legolas, and Gimli) travel across Middle-earth to help the kingdoms of men unite and fight against the massive armies of the Dark Lord Sauron and the traitorous wizard Saruman.

Climax

The heroic defense of Helm's Deep takes place, marking a massive turning point in the war as free peoples stand together against overwhelming darkness.
